1:1 Coaching

Working together, one to one.

Focused, personal time to work through whatever's taking up the most space in your life right now.

A little about the sessions

1:1 sessions are for anyone who wants space to think, be heard, and figure out what's next — without a fixed programme or a list of things to fix. Some people come with something specific on their mind. Others just need somewhere honest to start.

My approach is conversational, not clinical. We follow what's actually going on for you, rather than a script. It's grounded in the same values as the retreats and community work — connection, honesty, and giving yourself permission to slow down.

  • Format 1:1 sessions, in person or online
  • Suitable for Anyone wanting space to think, talk, and reconnect with themselves
  • Background Certified mindfulness & wellbeing practitioner, with a background in coaching and group facilitation since 2019
